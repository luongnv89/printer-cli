Brand Identity

printer-cli

Your printer's dashboard, in the terminal. A prompt and four ink tanks — the whole product in one glance.

Design concept

A terminal that knows its ink.

The rounded square reads as both a terminal window and the front of a printer. The green chevron is the shell prompt: everything printer-cli does starts from a command.

The four bars are the ink tanks from the prt tui dashboard, in cyan, magenta, yellow and key, each at a different level. They double as a signal-strength glyph, a nod to the network protocols underneath (IPP, eSCL, SNMP).

The wordmark is set in JetBrains Mono Bold and outlined to paths, lowercase to match the package name and the command line it lives on.
